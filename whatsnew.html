Introducing reDiffusion Pro

reDiffusion is now free to download and use. The free tier has everything you need for one source of live TV and one video library; Pro removes the limits on every device signed in with your Apple Account.

Plans

  • Monthly or yearly subscription — the yearly plan shows how much it saves against monthly, and you can switch between the two at any time from Settings → reDiffusion Pro.
  • Lifetime — a one-time purchase. To avoid paying twice, Lifetime becomes available once any running subscription has been cancelled (it keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for).
  • One purchase covers iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV, and supports Family Sharing. Use Restore Purchases on a new device if Pro doesn't appear by itself.

If a subscription ends

Nothing is deleted. Your tuners and libraries stay configured; the free tier simply keeps one tuner and one library switched on and you choose which. Restoring Pro switches everything back on.

iPhone Duo

  • On the inner display, where iOS hides the status bar, the guide now shows the time in its toolbar.
  • On the outer display in landscape, the guide, hero banner and channel column stay clear of the side toolbar in both rotations.

Apple TV

  • Settings now sits directly under the source picker in the sidebar, ahead of the category lists.
  • Returning from the Home screen or another app resumes playback instead of leaving the player on a spinner.
  • Guide and library artwork is shown whole rather than cropped.
  • The Continue Watching row follows the title you just played.

Everywhere

  • Refreshing a tuner, playlist or guide always fetches the latest copy; a change made moments ago on your server shows up straight away.
  • Files that end early (an episode whose recording was cut short) now play to their real end instead of stalling.
  • iPad: the live banner card in the guide expands on tap.

Privacy

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. reDiffusion learns only whether Pro is active; it never sees your payment details. See the privacy policy.
