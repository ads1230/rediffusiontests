reDiffusion Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026

reDiffusion is a TV guide and media player for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV. It plays live television and video from sources you configure: network tuners on your home network, playlists you add, and your own media servers. This policy explains what the app stores, where it goes, and what we can and cannot see.

The short version

  • reDiffusion has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Your settings, sources, sign-in details and watch history stay on your devices and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own iCloud. We cannot read them.
  • The app contacts your sources, a small number of services we operate for guide data and artwork, and only those third-party services you choose to enable.
  • We do not sell, share or collect personal data.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following on each device where you use it:

  • Your sources: tuner addresses, playlist and guide URLs, media-server addresses, and the categories and channel matches you set up.
  • Sign-in details for your media servers and for any optional services you enable. These are stored in the device Keychain, Apple's encrypted credential store.
  • Watch progress, watched episodes, favourites, reminders and downloaded episodes.
  • Cached guide data, artwork and the app's preferences.

All of this is removed when you delete the app (downloads and caches) or when you remove the source inside the app. Keychain items are removed when you sign out of a source or delete the app's data.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you enable iCloud sync, your sources, preferences, watch state and sign-in details are stored in the private CloudKit database of your own Apple Account and in your iCloud Keychain, so your other devices pick them up. Apple holds that data under its own privacy policy; it is encrypted to your account and we have no access to it. Turn sync off in Settings at any time; the data stays in your iCloud until you remove it or delete the app's iCloud data from your Apple Account settings.

Notifications

Programme reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. The app uses Apple's push service only to keep iCloud sync up to date between your devices; those pushes carry no content.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting services of our own. If you share diagnostics with Apple, that is governed by Apple's settings on your device.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data.
  • No data processing on our servers beyond answering the requests described above.

Children

reDiffusion is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. The app plays whatever sources an adult configures.

Your rights

Because your data lives on your devices and in your own iCloud, you control it directly: edit or remove sources in the app, sign out of services, turn off iCloud sync, or delete the app. For data held by a third-party service you enabled, contact that provider. Under the UK GDPR and equivalent laws you may ask us what information our servers hold about a request; in practice this is limited to short-lived server logs. Contact details are below.
