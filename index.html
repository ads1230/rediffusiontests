reDiffusion

A TV guide and player for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV that works with your own sources: HDHomeRun and SAT>IP tuners, M3U playlists with XMLTV guides, and your Jellyfin, Plex or Emby media servers. reDiffusion provides no content of its own.

Getting started

  • Live TV: Settings → Tuners → Add. Pick your tuner type or paste a playlist URL. Then Settings → Guides → Add and paste an XMLTV guide URL, or use the guide your tuner publishes.
  • Films and series: Settings → VOD Sources → Add, then enter your media server's address and sign-in.
  • Several devices: with reDiffusion Pro, turn on iCloud sync in Settings and your sources follow you to your other devices.

Free and Pro

reDiffusion is free to use with one live TV tuner and one video library. reDiffusion Pro (monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase) adds unlimited tuners and libraries and iCloud sync, on every device signed in with your Apple Account, with Family Sharing. Manage or restore it in Settings → reDiffusion Pro. If a subscription ends nothing is deleted; the free tier keeps one tuner and one library switched on until you restore Pro. See what's new.
